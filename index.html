hermes-box

A personal assistant's access to its own single mailbox. One user, no other accounts.

Privacy

Mail is read and sent only for that one account, on the owner's request. Message content is passed to the AI model provider only as needed to carry out that request. No data is sold or used for advertising or any other purpose.

hermes-box's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account permissions.
